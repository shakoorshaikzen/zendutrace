# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Fleet managers, cold-chain/logistics operations leads, and supply-chain directors evaluating asset-tracking hardware. They arrive skeptical, comparing against hardwired GPS trackers they already run (Samsara, Motive, legacy telematics). Decision context: desk research, often on a work laptop, forwarding links to colleagues. Resellers and channel reps are a secondary audience.

## Product Purpose

Marketing landing page for XenTag™ (peel-and-stick BLE + cellular smart tracking labels) and XenAuth™ (NFC tap-to-verify authentication tags), both by ZenduIT. Positioning: tracking for high-value / high-security goods (pharma, electronics, art, evidence) — a physical paper label that gets tracked, priced at $6/label with tracking included. The page's job: make "it's a label on paper that tracks things" land within 2 seconds, prove it with real footage and real platform screenshots, and convert via the free-sample offer. Success = demo bookings and free-label claims. ZenduONE is deliberately not named — the page stays focused on the label product.

## Brand Personality

Industrial confidence. Expert, field-proven, no-nonsense — evidence over decoration. Numbers carry the argument (3,625 thefts, $35B spoiled, -2.0°C, $8/label); the tone is a seasoned ops engineer, not a startup pitch deck. Peer reference: Samsara's product-led directness.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated SaaS landing pages: uppercase tracked eyebrow labels on every section, identical icon+heading+text card grids, decorative glassmorphism, gradient text.
- Startup-cute copy ("supercharge", "unleash", emoji bullets).
- Consumer-gadget gloss; this is industrial B2B.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the product working** — real footage of the label being applied and real platform screenshots beat animations; fabricated dashboards and made-up reviews are banned.
2. **Numbers are the voice** — concrete stats ($6/label, 1-year battery, 29 countries) anchor every claim.
3. **Field-real over polished-generic** — warehouse/freight texture, not abstract illustrations.
4. **Label-first story** — the physical label is the product; the platform is supporting proof, not the headline.
5. **Earn the CTA** — proof (real film, comparison, real client logos, incidents) before the ask.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target set by owner (visual polish prioritized). Existing prefers-reduced-motion handling on marquees should be preserved.
